\hypertarget{FBSDEs}{
    \subsection{Multi-Period Compliance MFG-FBSDEs with DL \normalfont \hfill RA \hspace{70pt} 2024.05 - Present}}
    
\subsubsection{Columbia University Stats Department}
\vspace{3pt}
% \begin{adjustwidth}{10pt}{}
    Supervised by Prof. Campbell, I set up NN models with Deep FBSDE Solvers for the multi-period MFG in the REC markets, as an extended topic to the single-period model. In the next step, we will make it more realistic by adding more periods and inventory carryforward (or expiration) rules to the current framework.
% \end{adjustwidth}
\begin{itemize}
    \item\begin{description}
        \item[Innovative Numeric Tricks] We proposed 3 tricks for greater stability and better convergence:
    \end{description}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Clamping - restrict values within $[0,1]$ and avoid gradient explosion.
        \item Sigmoid Approximation - increase continuity and differentiability.
        \item Logit Transformation - map $[0,1]$ to $\mathbb{R}$ while providing differentiability.
    \end{itemize}
    \item\begin{description}
        \item[Experimenting And Tuning] Experimented with various combos of optimizers, schedulers, and loss functions, tuning parameters like learning rate penalty weights for stable convergence. 
    \end{description}
    \item \begin{description}
        \item[Benchmarking] Proposed Separate Optimization as a benchmark, which could be interpreted as a short-sighted perspective against long-term planning of agents in the Joint Optimization.
    \end{description}
    \item \begin{description}
        \item[Documentation, Typesetting, and GitHub-Publishing] Wrapped the models into well-structured packages and composed READMEs for code instructions. Summarized the period results into an \href{https://github.com/OrangeAoo/Multi-Compliance-MFG-FBSDEs/blob/FBSDE/FinalReports/ReportOverview-MFG-FBSDE.pdf}{Overview Report}. All works are documented and made public in \MFG.  
    \end{description}
\end{itemize}
